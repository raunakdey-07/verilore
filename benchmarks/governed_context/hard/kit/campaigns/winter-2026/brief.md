# Winter 2026 campaign

The winter campaign brings Kashmiri Kahwa to the front. It launches on
1 December 2026 and runs until 31 January 2027.

## Offer

- The winter offer is 15% off the Kashmiri Kahwa tin.
- The winter offer ends on 31 January 2027.
- The winter offer does not apply to gift boxes.

## Audience

- Write the winter campaign for people who already drink tea every day.
- Lead with the saffron, not the discount.
