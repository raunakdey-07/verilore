Every line below is current and approved. Drafts, rejected and replaced statements are not included. Do not contradict these lines.

What your AI sees is what you approved.

And you can prove it later.

In Python, write(task, your_model) runs brief, draft, check and one revision. Over MCP, an agent's writes wait as drafts until a person approves them.

$ verilore --help

usage: verilore <command> [options]

brief
Print approved memory as plain lines, ready to paste into a model's prompt.
check
Check a draft against approved memory: banned words, wrong and made-up numbers.
review
List what a person should settle before memory reaches a model.
recall
Answer a question from memory, with its source and the time it was true.
history
Show what this memory said before, and what replaced it.
verify
Verify a Memory Receipt offline: no server, database, model or network.
